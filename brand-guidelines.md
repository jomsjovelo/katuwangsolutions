### 🔠 Brand Typography (Fonts)

I recommend using **Inter** or **Montserrat** (available on Google Fonts) for all web and app interfaces.

**1. Primary Heading Font (Used for 'KATUWANG')**
* **Font Family:** Inter or Montserrat
* **Weight:** 800 (Extra-Bold) or 900 (Black)
* **Style:** ALL CAPS
* **Usage:** Main titles, big headers, and the primary brand name.

**2. Secondary Subheading Font (Used for 'SOLUTIONS')**
* **Font Family:** Inter or Montserrat
* **Weight:** 400 (Regular) or 500 (Medium)
* **Style:** ALL CAPS with **Wide Letter-Spacing** (Tracking)
* **Usage:** Subtitles, descriptors, and secondary text below main headers.
